name: App visual effects
description: User's app-wide constraint on hover motion and glass styling.

Do not introduce hover animations that lift or enlarge cards, or glass-style blur and transparency, anywhere in this app.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested removal throughout the entire app and confirmed both effects in the clarification.

**How to apply:** Keep new or redesigned surfaces opaque and stationary on hover. This does not require removing functional loading indicators or modal dimming overlays.
